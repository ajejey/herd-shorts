# Storylines: three sample Shorts

_8 Nov 2026. One sample video per format from SHORTS_STRATEGY.md, each built on real
player data (read-only from production, saved in data.json). Pick one storyline per video._

Shared assumptions: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 15-22 s each. Music bed + a few sound effects,
no voiceover. No paid items. Brand from the live site: Fredoka 700, brown ink #2D1810,
cream #FFF8E7, the meadow sky (pale blue to butter yellow), green #3D8B5A, Pink Cow
pink #E84A8B, the cow mascot and the herd sheep (real SVGs from the site).

---

## Video A: "Can you beat the herd?" (Daily Guesstimate, 7 Nov)

Data: "Roughly how many times can a US dollar bill be folded back and forth before it
tears?" Answer **4,000**. 116 players. Middle guess **25**. 64% guessed under 100.
**1 player** landed within 10%.

### A1. The herd on the number line (recommended: the herd made visible is ours alone, and the jump from 25 to 4,000 is felt, not read)
- Device / form: a number lands / a flight along one world.
- What you see: 116 little sheep drop onto a number line at their real guesses and pile
  up around 25. The camera races right, past 100 and past 1,000, to 4,000, where one
  sheep stands alone. You feel how wrong everyone was, including you.
- First 2 s: the question in giant type over an empty number line, "Guess before the
  herd does" and a 3-second countdown already running.
- Beats: question → countdown → the herd drops in, piling at 25 → "The answer is…" →
  camera whips to 4,000 → "1 of 116 got close" → end card with the cow: "Play Daily
  Guesstimate".
- Look: meadow sky ground, brown line-art sheep, Fredoka 700; pink only on the answer flag.
- Sound: bouncy pizzicato bed (~110 bpm), soft pops as the herd lands, one whoosh on
  the camera run, a riser into 4,000.

### A2. Fold it
- Device / form: the feature as the hook / one continuous macro take.
- What you see: a dollar bill folding back and forth with a counter. At 25, "Most players
  stopped here". It keeps going, the counter flies to 4,000, and the bill tears.
- First 2 s: a dollar bill mid-fold filling the frame, counter at 1, the question above.
- Beats: fold, fold → "most stopped at 25" → accelerating folds → tear at 4,000 → end card.
- Look: a real photo of a bill on a wooden desk, warm light, Fredoka counter.
- Sound: a paper crinkle per fold, speeding up; a tear on the payoff.

### A3. You vs the herd (the bold one)
- Device / form: a question with the input / object-to-object chain.
- What you see: the real Daily Guesstimate screen. A guess of 25 is typed and submitted,
  and the result opens into the number line with the true answer.
- First 2 s: the game screen with the question large and a blinking cursor in the guess box.
- Beats: question → type 25 → submit → "The herd said 25, the answer is 4,000" → end card.
- Look: the site's own UI, cream and pink buttons.
- Sound: soft key ticks, a thunk on submit, a riser to the reveal.

## Video B: "America answered" (Top Answers)

Data: "Name something that always breaks in an office." 3,071 answers. Printer 38%,
Computer 8%, Mugs 8%, Stapler 6%, Chair 5%, Coffee machine 5%, Copier 4%, Pencil 4%.

### B1. The board (recommended: it's the game they land on when they click, and the blank board makes everyone guess)
- Device / form: a question with the input / one frame that rebuilds.
- What you see: our real Top Answers board, eight blank pink-numbered tiles. Answers
  flip in from #8 up. The #1 tile, PRINTER 38%, takes over the screen.
- First 2 s: the question in giant type over eight blank tiles, "3,071 people answered.
  Can you name #1?"
- Beats: question → 3 s to guess → #8 to #2 flip in → pause → PRINTER 38% fills the
  frame → end card: "Play Top Answers".
- Look: cream card, pink #E84A8B numerals, Fredoka 700, the board as on the site.
- Sound: upbeat game-show bed (~120 bpm), a tile flip per answer (three takes rotated),
  a riser and impact on the printer.

### B2. The office
- Device / form: a moment in someone's day / a flight through one world.
- What you see: Monday, 9:02 AM. The camera tracks across a flat-illustrated office.
  The mug cracks (8%), the stapler jams (6%), a chair wheel rolls off (5%), and the
  printer at the end flashes PAPER JAM: 38%.
- First 2 s: a clock reading 9:02 on an office wall, the question in giant type.
- Beats: question → desk to desk, each object breaks with its % → printer jams → end card.
- Look: flat vector office in the brand palette, brown outlines.
- Sound: office hum, a crack or clunk per object, a printer grind on the payoff.

### B3. Countdown slam (the bold one)
- Device / form: type carries the argument / cut on the beat.
- What you see: each answer slams in full-screen in giant type on its own colour field,
  #8 up to #1. PRINTER 38% fills the screen.
- First 2 s: "Name something that ALWAYS breaks in an office", with ALWAYS in pink.
- Beats: question → 7 slams on the beat → printer → end card.
- Look: hard-cut colour fields from the brand palette, giant Fredoka 700.
- Sound: driving bed (~128 bpm), a cut on every beat, a crash on the printer.

## Video C: "Where did America land?" (Spectrum dial with Hot Takes data, 4 Nov)

Data: "Pineapple on pizza?" 7,683 players. Absolutely 3,981 (52%), Never 3,702 (48%).

### C1. The dial (recommended: it's the Wavelength dial people already know from TikTok, and it's our Spectrum game)
- Device / form: a number lands / one frame that rebuilds.
- What you see: a big Spectrum dial from "Absolutely" to "Never". The needle swings
  hard both ways, then settles just left of centre at 52 / 48.
- First 2 s: "Pineapple on pizza?" giant, the dial below with the needle already
  swinging, and "7,683 people answered. Where did they land?"
- Beats: question → "comment your guess" → needle swings → settles → "52% Absolutely,
  48% Never: split almost in half" → end card: "Pick your side: Daily Hot Takes".
- Look: meadow ground; dial halves green #3D8B5A and pink #E84A8B; a pizza slice as
  the needle's hub; Fredoka 700.
- Sound: playful bed, ratchet ticks as the needle swings, a riser, an impact as it settles.

### C2. Tug of war
- Device / form: a character does the work / one continuous take.
- What you see: two teams of our cows tug a rope over a giant pizza. The rope inches back
  and forth and stops at 52-48.
- First 2 s: the two teams already pulling, "Pineapple on pizza?" above.
- Beats: question → tug → stall → settle at 52/48 → end card.
- Look: the site's cow mascot, multiplied, on the meadow.
- Sound: effort grunts, rope creaks, a cheer at the settle.

### C3. Split screen (the bold one)
- Device / form: life split in two / split screen to one.
- What you see: the screen splits into Absolutely and Never. 7,683 tiny pizza slices rain
  into the two halves while counters run. The divider gets shoved to 52/48, then the two
  halves fold into one line.
- First 2 s: the split already raining, "Pineapple on pizza?" across the middle.
- Beats: question → rain fills both sides → counters race → divider settles → fold to
  one line → end card.
- Look: two flat colour halves, green and pink, with little pizza slices.
- Sound: a rising patter of pops, an impact on the final split.
